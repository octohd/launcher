# Code of Conduct

OctoHD is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on improving OctoHD and helping its users.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to **octohd@proton.me**. Include only the information needed to understand the incident, and do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

This policy applies in all OctoHD project spaces and whenever someone represents the project publicly.
